My stocks

Enter the NSE symbol, your buy price and quantity, then tap Add. Add a target price to get a notification when the price reaches or goes above it (repeated at your "Repeat alert after" setting). These stocks make up your Portfolio and the "My stocks" section of the Watchlist.

Price check frequency

How often prices are fetched, in the background and while the app is open. Every tab and the home-screen widgets update at this pace. 15 and 30 seconds use more battery and data.

Background notification

Android shows one silent notification while Wealthora checks prices in the background. It no longer changes after each check. To remove it completely, tap the button and switch it off. Your price and target alerts keep working.

Home-screen widgets

Long-press an empty spot on your home screen → Widgets → scroll to Wealthora. Add Wealthora Portfolio (value and profit / loss) or Wealthora Watchlist (scrollable, green / red by today's move). Tap ↻ on a widget to refresh it.
