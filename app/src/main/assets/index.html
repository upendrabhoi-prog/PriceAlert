My stocks

Enter the NSE symbol, your buy price and quantity, then tap Add. Add a target price to get a notification when the price reaches or goes above it (repeated at your "Repeat alert after" setting). These stocks make up your Portfolio and the "My stocks" section of the Watchlist.

Price check frequency

How often prices are fetched, in the background and while the app is open. Every tab and the home-screen widgets update at this pace. 15 and 30 seconds use more battery and data.

Background notification

Android shows one silent notification while Wealthora checks prices in the background. It no longer changes after each check. To remove it completely, tap the button and switch it off. Your price and target alerts keep working.

Home-screen widgets

Long-press an empty spot on your home screen → Widgets → scroll to Wealthora. Add any of: Wealthora Portfolio (value and profit / loss), Wealthora Watchlist (green / red by today's move), Wealthora Distance from buy price (top 10 closest to your buy price) and Wealthora % Target left (smallest % to target first). Widgets update at your price check frequency; tap ↻ on a widget to refresh it now.

Market hours onlyPrices are checked automatically only Mon–Fri, 9:00 AM – 3:30 PM IST, to save battery. Outside these hours the last prices are shown.

Google Sheet · Realised trades

Paste the link of your Google Sheet. The Realised tab reads your sold trades from it and checks it again for changes automatically.

Sheet (tab) name Check the sheet for changes every
In Google Sheets tap Share → General access → Anyone with the link → Viewer, so the app can read it (no Google sign-in needed). The first row with these headings is used, in any order: Stock Name Account Date of Selling Buy Price Sell Price Quantity Profit/Loss. Dates as DD-MM-YYYY.
